My Favorite Places in the Twin Cities Area

  • Free Geek Twin Cities — A nice place with lots of old and retro technology. I picked up a CRT monitor here freshman year.
  • Parade Ice Garden — Where I go to ice skate. It's also right next to the Sculpture Garden, and there's a nice coffee shop nearby.
  • Twin City Model Railroad Museum — I like trains.
  • Stone Arch Bridge — A beautiful view of the river and the mill ruins. This is a must-see if you ever visit the Twin Cities.
  • Como Park — Great nature and a lovely conservatory. They even have tropical plants here! If you visit in spring, you can see beautiful cherry blossoms.
